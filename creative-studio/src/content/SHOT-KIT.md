# DeliciousDuck Duck-Breast Short-Form Shot Kit

Phone defaults: portrait 9:16, lock exposure/white balance, rear main camera, 4K/30 for static instruction and 4K/60 for pour/slice/fat-bead moments. Use soft window light from the side, switch off orange overhead lamps, and keep the background quiet and dark-neutral. Capture five seconds before and after every action. Record clean room/kitchen sound, but never treat it as required narration.

## Video assignment

### Start It Cold

Use scoring, cold-pan placement, first fat beads, pooling, pouring, edge colour, probe, rest, and slice. The opening should contrast a quiet cold placement with the expected aggressive hiss. Do not add a synthetic hiss.

### Crisp Skin, Finally

Capture optional diagnostic alternates: visibly damp skin before blotting; two pieces nearly touching, then separated; pooled fat before pour-off; correct skin-up rest. Do not intentionally ruin food merely for a failure shot—use safe, honest setup comparisons.

### Where To Probe

Prioritize side-profile breast geometry, probe entering horizontally, a second angle showing the thickest centre, and the display settling. Record the reading but treat the source-page safety context—not colour—as the editorial authority.

## Replaceable template slots

Every video currently uses approved stills and illustrations. Replace scene media only when the phone clip matches the scene truth, framing, and start/end state above. Retain the still fallback for footage that is shaky, incorrectly lit, unsafe, or visually ambiguous.

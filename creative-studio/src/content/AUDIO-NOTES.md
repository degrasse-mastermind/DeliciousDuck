# Audio Direction — Duck Breast Field Notes

All three masters are delivered silent with complete on-screen text. Narration scripts, JSON captions and SRT files are render-package companions, not embedded synthetic voice tracks.

## Shared music mood

Choose a properly licensed instrumental bed with tactile restraint: brushed percussion, low upright-bass or muted-pluck pulse, sparse piano/felt keys, and little harmonic clutter. Target roughly 84–98 BPM with enough negative space for narration. The feeling is premium culinary editorial—quiet confidence, not restaurant luxury, slapstick, or trend-chasing.

Avoid ukulele lifestyle cues, trap hi-hat clichés, corporate uplift, giant trailer rises, meme drops, aggressive whooshes, and music that implies frantic cooking. Confirm platform and commercial-use licensing before publishing; no music is bundled in this sprint.

## Sound-design policy

Use real sound only when it was captured with the matching real clip. Never place an invented sizzle under a still illustration or imply that the current editorial motion was filmed in the DeliciousDuck kitchen. Clean typography/image transitions need no whoosh.

Potential future real sounds:

- knife moving through cold fat during scoring
- quiet contact as skin meets a genuinely cold pan
- gradual first sizzle as heat rises
- rendered fat pouring into a heatproof vessel
- thermometer button/read confirmation where naturally captured
- knife through crisp rested skin

Keep every effect low and specific. Preserve a half-second of room tone around replacement clips so edits do not sound cut out.

## Per-video rhythm

### Start It Cold

Begin nearly silent to make the absence of an opening hiss part of the lesson. Introduce a low, patient pulse as the “cold renders first” contrast appears; allow real gradual sizzle only with future matching footage.

### Crisp Skin, Finally

Use a restrained diagnostic cadence: one soft percussive marker per cause, no alarm or failure buzzer. The recap may add a slightly fuller pulse without becoming celebratory noise.

### Where To Probe

Keep the bed precise and spacious. A single muted tick may mark the side-entry annotation and safety-context scene; avoid medical-monitor or warning-siren tropes.

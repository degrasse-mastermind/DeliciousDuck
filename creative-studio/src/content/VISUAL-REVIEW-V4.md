# V4 Visual Acceptance Review

## Decision

Accepted for commit review after two still-render passes and one revised video pass. Technical QA followed this visual review.

## Pinterest

The first V4 pass was rejected because five Pins reused one pan photograph with nearly identical crops. The accepted pass assigns explicit compositions and crop positions. The set now reads as one publication while separating reference, technical, decision, troubleshooting, counterintuitive method, checklist, recipe outcome, technique, and beginner-confidence jobs.

## Carousels

The crisp-skin carousel uses photography for sensory proof, an approved technical illustration for scoring depth, and a finished-result recap. The doneness carousel uses photography for appetite/context and the approved probe illustration for placement and factual temperature panels. No slide uses the old sliced-duck or thermometer sketches.

## Short-form video

All masters open on food or technical proof within the first frame, change scene every four seconds, work muted, and finish on food proof plus one CTA. Logo-only endings were rejected and removed. Where to Probe is technical-illustration led; the other masters use animated approved photography because no approved genuine cooking footage exists. No synthetic footage or kitchen sound is presented as captured.

## Covers, crops, and reference

Text and clean covers retain a recognizable food or technical focal point in center 4:5 and square crops. The four temperature-reference formats preserve the distinction between culinary conventions and the USDA poultry minimum, with factual text rendered deterministically.

## Remaining production opportunity

Future real capture of a genuinely cold pan, pooled rendered fat, wet-versus-dry skin, and skin-up resting would increase motion and diagnostic specificity. Those capture slots remain fail-closed and are not required by or used in this V4 batch.

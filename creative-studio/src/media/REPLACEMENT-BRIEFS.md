# Approved Illustration Library — Replacement Briefs

## 1. Technical probe placement

- **Subject:** Skin-on duck breast in clean sectional/three-quarter side view with a slim instant-read probe entering horizontally.
- **Purpose:** Make the correct entry path and thickest-part centre immediately legible.
- **Composition:** Breast occupies the right 55–60%; uninterrupted negative space left/top for headline and deterministic arrows.
- **Camera equivalent:** 20–30° elevated side view, not overhead.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Distinguish skin, fat cap and muscle; probe tip ends in the geometric centre of the thickest muscle; never pass down through fat or touch pan/board.
- **Palette:** Ivory ground, forest/charcoal contours, restrained golden fat, muted cranberry muscle accent.
- **Line/texture:** Controlled primary contour; thin anatomical separators; minimal hatch only at cut surfaces.
- **Safe text area:** Left 42% and upper-left quadrant clear.
- **Crop/orientation:** 4:5 master with safe 9:16 extension and square centre crop.
- **Required accuracy:** Highest; chef/food-safety editorial review required.
- **Motion:** Yes—separate breast, probe, probe path and target layers.

## 2. Technical scoring anatomy

- **Subject:** Cold skin-on duck breast showing parallel or diamond scoring and a small edge cutaway.
- **Purpose:** Show that cuts pass through skin/fat but stop above muscle.
- **Composition:** Single breast on a minimal board plane; one enlarged cutaway inset at lower right.
- **Camera equivalent:** 35° overhead product-diagram view.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Consistent fat-cap depth; no cut line entering red muscle; plausible spacing and knife angle.
- **Palette:** Warm ivory, forest contours, pale gold fat, restrained cranberry only in cutaway muscle.
- **Line/texture:** Precise ink/vector hybrid; no loose pencil fill.
- **Safe text area:** Upper-left 40% clear; inset must remain inside centre 4:5 crop.
- **Crop/orientation:** 4:5 and 1:1; optional 9:16 extension.
- **Required accuracy:** High; technique review required.
- **Motion:** Yes—score lines and cutaway layers reveal independently.

## 3. Fat-flow and pan-contact mechanism

- **Subject:** Duck breast skin-side down in a heavy pan, with a restrained cutaway showing liquid fat leaving scored channels and a shallow pool interrupting contact.
- **Purpose:** Explain why pooled fat slows crisping and why pouring it off restores contact.
- **Composition:** Pan arc across lower third; breast centred-right; clear left text field; no jar or decorative pouring tableau.
- **Camera equivalent:** Low 25° pan-level view combined with a controlled technical cutaway.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Breast sits flat; pool depth is shallow and physically plausible; arrows describe flow without becoming infographic clutter.
- **Palette:** Forest/charcoal cookware, ivory ground, controlled gold fat, muted natural meat tones.
- **Line/texture:** Strong silhouette, minimal metal shading, crisp directional marks.
- **Safe text area:** Left 38–42% clear.
- **Crop/orientation:** 4:5 base; wide 16:9 mechanism version; 9:16 layered extension.
- **Required accuracy:** High; pan-contact physics must read correctly.
- **Motion:** Yes—separate fat beads, flow arrows, pool and breast/pan layers.

## 4. Cold-pan mechanism editorial plate

- **Subject:** One duck breast in a cold heavy pan with subtle visual contrast between “surface browns first” and “fat renders first.”
- **Purpose:** Explain the counterintuitive cold-pan principle without pretending to be cooking proof.
- **Composition:** Sophisticated editorial plate, single pan silhouette, paired micro-detail or split-state—not a cartoon before/after.
- **Camera equivalent:** 30° elevated pan view with macro inset.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Correct skin-side orientation, fat cap thickness and pan contact; no fake flames or steam.
- **Palette:** Dark forest pan field, ivory annotation space, gold fat accents, restrained cranberry warning state.
- **Line/texture:** Confident contour with selective material texture; no watercolor wash.
- **Safe text area:** Left/top 40% or full lower third depending orientation.
- **Crop/orientation:** 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 variants from one layered master.
- **Required accuracy:** High conceptual accuracy; editorial rather than literal time claim.
- **Motion:** Yes—layers support slow parallax and a controlled state reveal.

## 5. Thermometer reference object

- **Subject:** Premium, generic instant-read thermometer with slim probe; no invented manufacturer identity.
- **Purpose:** Serve as a clean supporting object in temperature-reference layouts.
- **Composition:** Strong diagonal silhouette, isolated on alpha/ivory, with display readable only when deterministic overlay supplies the value.
- **Camera equivalent:** Three-quarter product view.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Plausible hinge, probe thickness, display and buttons; no brand imitation.
- **Palette:** Forest/charcoal body, ivory face, restrained gold detail.
- **Line/texture:** Controlled product-illustration contours; almost no hatch.
- **Safe text area:** Object confined to right or lower-right 45%.
- **Crop/orientation:** Transparent master supporting portrait, landscape and square.
- **Required accuracy:** Medium-high; object must look functional and credible.
- **Motion:** Yes—probe and body separated for a restrained hinge/opening movement.

## 6. Carryover and doneness editorial anatomy

- **Subject:** Consistent duck-breast cross-section with restrained concentric heat migration and no exaggerated meat colour.
- **Purpose:** Explain pull versus final temperature and carryover without using colour as a safety proxy.
- **Composition:** One cross-section system reused across temperature carousel and reference variants; typography remains separate.
- **Camera equivalent:** Orthographic editorial section.
- **Anatomy constraints:** Stable proportions across frames; bands represent conceptual heat movement, not certified temperature zones.
- **Palette:** Ivory ground, forest contours, muted natural muscle, gold heat accents, cranberry reserved for explicit safety callout.
- **Line/texture:** Clean sectional drawing with minimal tonal modelling.
- **Safe text area:** Left 45% or top 35% clear depending layout.
- **Crop/orientation:** 4:5 master, landscape reference and 9:16 motion extension.
- **Required accuracy:** High conceptual clarity; all numeric values remain deterministic text overlays.
- **Motion:** Yes—heat/carryover bands separated for slow inward movement.

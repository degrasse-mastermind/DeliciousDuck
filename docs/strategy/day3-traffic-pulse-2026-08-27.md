**Subject line options**
1. Four questions. One duck plan.
2. The one card that tells you what matters

Answer four quick questions about the duck in front of you — get the card that has everything on it.

**Body copy**

> **Duck Game Plan**
>
> Most duck advice answers the wrong question — "how do you cook duck," in general. We built something narrower: what do you do with the exact bird in front of you, right now.
>
> Answer four questions — species, cut, weight, and how you're cooking it — and the Duck Game Plan gives you one card back: the real risk for that bird, the critical move, target temperature, resting time, cook timing, portions, and what to serve alongside. No scrolling a recipe to find the one number you actually need.
>
> We're also testing whether to build a Premium version — save your plan and get a printable PDF of it. Nothing is for sale yet. The first 100 people who join the waitlist (there's a link under every plan the tool generates) get it for $9 if we build it — everyone else pays more later, or it doesn't happen at all. Either way, joining just adds you to The Duck Drop and tells us to email you first.

**CTA button:** `Get your duck plan`
**Destination:** https://deliciousduck.com/tools/duck-game-plan

*(Optional sign-off, matching site tagline: "Better duck. A more delicious world.")*

**Notes**
- The "$9 for the first 100" line is not new marketing — it's the exact teaser copy already live on the site under every generated plan, so this email is just surfacing what a visitor already sees, not introducing a new claim.
- Kept to three short paragraphs — this is a nudge email, not a full newsletter issue.

## 2. Pinterest pin brief

**Pin title** (under 100 characters)
`Duck Game Plan: Temp, Timing, Resting & What to Serve` (55 characters)

**Pin description** (Pinterest-SEO-friendly, under ~500 characters)
`Answer four quick questions about your duck — species, cut, weight, cooking method — and get one reference card back: the real risk, the critical move, duck breast temperature, resting time, cook timing, portions, and what to serve. Built for roast duck, seared duck breast, confit and more. A free duck cooking tool from DeliciousDuck — no recipe-scrolling, just the numbers you need.`

**Image/text-overlay concept**
A kitchen-reference-card mockup matching the tool's actual output format — not a lifestyle food photo. A simple line-drawn duck breast or duck illustration at top, then stacked labelled fields down the card (Risk / Critical Move / Temp / Rest / Timing / Portions / Serve With) styled like an index card, in the site's existing typography/colors. Best version: an actual screenshot of a real generated plan card, so the numbers on the pin are genuine tool output, not invented placeholders. Small line at bottom: "4 questions. 1 card. deliciousduck.com."

**Destination URL:** https://deliciousduck.com/tools/duck-game-plan

**Suggested board:** "Duck Recipes & Cooking Tips" (or whichever existing DeliciousDuck board fits — check the account before creating a new one). Pinterest category: Food & Drink > Main Dishes.

**Notes**
- No affiliate link, no price claim needed on the pin itself — the $9/waitlist detail lives on-site once someone clicks through, so the pin can stay focused on the free tool.
